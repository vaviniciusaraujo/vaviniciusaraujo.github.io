I’m Vinícius Araújo, a 3D Artist and Senior Designer from Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, with nearly two decades of experience across 3D, graphic and motion design. I’m currently part of the team at ManvsMachine™ in Los Angeles.

Over the years I’ve worked with a wide range of clients — especially in music, shaping visuals for concerts and live performances. But what has truly defined my path is a constant focus on personal projects and visual research: instead of a portfolio built around conventional client work, mine is built around experiments that express how I see things.

Art first, problem second.

Design, for me, is more than a set of technical solutions — it’s a space for exploration, aesthetics and creative thinking. Letting intuition lead, every project becomes a way to explore new visual territory and share how I see the world.
